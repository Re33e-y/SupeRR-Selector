What SupeRR Selector does

SupeRR Selector is the Rajasthan Royals Analytics Department's player evaluation pipeline for Season 2. It scores every delivery by how much it can swing the match, then rates players with a regression that separates baseline talent from the response to pressure.

Pre-ball LightGBM state machine

WP model

A LightGBM classifier reads the match state before each delivery and returns the batting side's win probability. Leverage Index (LI) is the expected swing in that probability from the ball about to be bowled.

Data hygiene

3 fixes
1

len1 leak fix

A strict pre-ball feature cutoff. Nothing that is only known after the delivery can enter the model.

2

Super Overs removed

Tie-break dynamics distort LI, so those deliveries are dropped from training and scoring.

3

DLS targets removed

Win probability assumptions break on adjusted targets, so rain-affected matches are excluded.

Crossed ridge regression

Every player's rating splits into baseline talent and a pressure adjustment.

Why rank on lo10

Resample by match

200 replicates, each drawing whole matches so ball-level correlation stays intact.

2

Take the 10th percentile

A conservative floor on skill. Nine in ten resamples score at or above it.

Variance penalty

Consistent beats lucky. Thin samples sit further below their P, as the batter chart shows.

Metric architecture

Two upgrades are planned. Pick one to compare.

Phase-relative leverage

PRL = LI ÷ Phase-Specific Variance

A 2.5% swing in the Powerplay is routine. The same swing in the Death Overs is rare. Dividing by the phase's own variance keeps only the pressure that is unusual for that phase.
